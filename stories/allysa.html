WEEK 7 · MATCHUP 3 · THE PREGAME
“SURVIVOR ON THE MOON” VS. THE PROFESSIONAL VICTIM
Team InfinityBrocklet vs. Wanda WacksEmOff — One Trusts NASA. The Other Doesn't Trust Trump. Neither Can Explain What the Fuck Is Going On.

Mike (2–4) genuinely believes mankind landed on the moon in 1969.

Of course he does. The government said it happened, so apparently that's good enough for Mike.

Think about it. Since 1969, we've invented smartphones, artificial intelligence, self-driving cars, and computers powerful enough to fit in our pockets.

Yet somehow, after all those technological advancements, we're supposed to believe we just forgot how to send people back to the fucking moon?

WAKE UP, MIKE.

And speaking of rockets, it doesn't take a rocket scientist to figure out that Team InfinityBrocklet's season is heading straight toward the ground.

At 2–4, Mike has scored 905.34 points while facing one of the league's toughest schedules. He's actually putting up respectable numbers, but apparently winning football games requires something NASA couldn't manufacture: a little fucking luck.

Across the field stands Allysa (4–2), manager of Wanda WacksEmOff, leading the league with 982.78 points and carrying enough confidence to make you think she invented fantasy football.

Allysa isn't nearly as trusting of politicians, especially Donald Trump.

Trump could promise her a $5,000 stimulus check, and she'd assume the fine print involved selling her soul, surrendering her voting rights, and personally thanking him on national television.

Meanwhile, Mike would probably ask NASA whether the check was being delivered from the International Space Station.

One believes the government can land astronauts on the moon. The other wouldn't trust it to deliver a fucking envelope.

And somewhere in Washington, politicians from both parties are doing what they do best: making promises with money that doesn't belong to them.

But if those $5,000 checks ever show up, don't forget to pay your fucking league dues, motherfuckers.

Last week, Allysa nearly lost to Jake, a man whose entire season has produced one victory and enough embarrassment to qualify for federal disaster assistance.

She escaped, but the performance raised legitimate questions about whether the league's highest-scoring team is actually as dominant as she thinks.

Of course, if she loses this week, expect a full investigation into Trump, inflation, government spending, and why none of those things explain her fantasy team's performance.

WEEK 7: THE NUMBERS DON'T LIE

PROJECTED: ALLYSA 148.38 | MIKE 121.32

WIN ODDS: ALLYSA 68% | MIKE 32%

Mike opened Survivor with five consecutive successful picks before finally missing one in Week 6, forcing him to use his first re-buy.

Five straight correct picks is genuinely impressive. In fact, Mike probably has a better chance of winning the entire Survivor competition than mankind ever had of setting foot on the fucking moon.

His fantasy record, however, tells a less flattering story. Despite scoring 905.34 points, an unforgiving schedule has turned respectable performances into losses.

Allysa has been considerably more successful, leading the league in scoring and entering the matchup with a projected 27.06-point advantage.

COMMISSIONER'S ANALYSIS & VERDICT

Mike has enough firepower to pull off the upset, and his record doesn't tell the whole story.

Allysa has the stronger season résumé, but her narrow escape against Jake suggests this matchup could be considerably closer than the projections indicate.

OFFICIAL PICK: WANDA WACKS HIM OFF BY 5 POINTS.

If Mike wins, he'll take one small step toward saving his season.

If he loses, he'll fall to 2–5, and the only thing less believable than his championship aspirations will be any Apollo mission.

Houston, we have a fucking problem.
