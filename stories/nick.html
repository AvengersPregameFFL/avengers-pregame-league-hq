WEEK 7 · MATCHUP 1 · THE PREGAME
THE PREGAME: NICK'S LUCK MEETS THE MOTHERFUCKING JUGS
Six weeks of divine intervention, a historically soft schedule, and one undefeated man who apparently sold his soul for a $50 fantasy football championship.

Six weeks. Six wins. Zero losses.

Somewhere, a terminally ill child just found out their Make-A-Wish request was denied because Nick apparently used up the entire department's miracle budget on fantasy football.

That's right. Nick is 6–0.

The man has accomplished something truly remarkable: convincing an entire league he's an unstoppable powerhouse while his opponents take turns shitting themselves on national television.

The League Luck Committee has officially launched an investigation, and frankly, the numbers are more disturbing than Nick's search history.

EXHIBIT A: SIX WEEKS OF OPPONENTS FLATLINING

Nick has allowed just 707.26 points, the fewest in the league.

His opponents average 117.88 points per game, compared with 140.11 points scored per game by the other seven teams.

That's 22.23 points of weekly schedule assistance.

Twenty-two fucking points.

At this point, Nick isn't managing a fantasy football team. He's running a hospice where opposing championship aspirations go to die.

And then there's Week 5.

Nick scored 101.76 points and WON.

One hundred and one.

That's not a fantasy football victory. That's a crime scene where both teams should have been pronounced dead, but the referee accidentally awarded Nick a pulse.

The committee has recommended that the league stop congratulating Nick and start conducting toxicology screenings on his opponents.

MEANWHILE, DK HAS BEEN PERFORMING OPEN-HEART SURGERY WITH A BUTTER KNIFE.

While Nick has been receiving victories like a spoiled child collecting participation trophies, DK has been dealing with an actual quarterback disaster.

First came Justin Herbert, the quarterback DK drafted to lead his offense.

Then came Jaxson Dart.

Both went down, leaving the JUGS quarterback room looking like the aftermath of a workplace accident that OSHA would prefer nobody investigated.

Most fantasy managers would have folded. Filed for disability. Started telling everyone they were concentrating on next season.

DK went shopping in the fucking morgue.

Out of the undrafted quarterback graveyard came Jordan Love and Aaron Rodgers.

Two quarterbacks nobody thought were worth drafting. Two discarded pieces DK somehow turned into a functioning offense.

And the JUGS kept winning.

That's not luck. That's a goddamn coaching masterclass.

Nick has spent six weeks watching his opponents die of natural causes. DK has been dragging his roster out of the ICU, shocking it back to life, and sending it straight back onto the football field.

One man has been handed wins.

The other has been manufacturing them out of spare parts, stubbornness, and pure fucking spite.

WEEK 7: THE FUNERAL ARRANGEMENTS HAVE BEEN MADE.

Now the undefeated Flash 23 Blast prepares to face Juggernauts Jumbo Package.

Nick averages 153.47 points per game.

The JUGS average 161.55.

For the first time all season, Nick may have to survive an opponent who doesn't immediately collapse like a Victorian child with tuberculosis.

League analysts are calling it the toughest examination of Nick's undefeated record.

The Luck Committee is calling it a scheduled execution of a fraudulent dynasty.

And DK?

DK isn't coming to congratulate Nick on six wins.

He's coming to put that undefeated record in a pine box, nail the lid shut, and charge Nick's family for the fucking funeral.

Of course, there's always the possibility that Nick gets lucky again.

Jordan Love could spontaneously combust. Aaron Rodgers could disappear into a cave to consult a dead shaman. The entire JUGS roster could contract dysentery during warmups.

Considering Nick's schedule so far, those are legitimate concerns.

But analysts are predicting something different this week.

THE MOTHERFUCKING JUGS ARE COMING.

And if anyone can finally drag Nick's undefeated record behind the woodshed and put it out of its misery, it's DK and the JUGS.

OFFICIAL LUCK COMMITTEE VERDICT

PREDICTION: JUGS WIN. NICK LOSES. SIX WEEKS OF DIVINE INTERVENTION END IN A CLOSED-CASKET FUNERAL.

Nick's 6–0 record remains officially recognized.

His claim to being the league's best manager remains unsupported by the committee.

And his apparent arrangement with God remains under investigation.

The Pregame Bugle has reached out to the Almighty for comment. His representatives confirmed that even He is getting tired of bailing Nick's ass out.

FINAL WARNING: Nick, enjoy that undefeated record while you can.

Because the JUGS aren't coming to break your winning streak.

THEY'RE COMING TO BURY THE FUCKING THING.
