WEEK 7 · MATCHUP 4 · THE PREGAME
THE 1–5 FAVORITE VS. THE 3–3 FRAUD
One Has Multiple Fantasy Championships. The Other Has a 201-Point Game. Neither Has Much to Celebrate This Year.

Hunter used to manage people. Now he can't even manage a winning fantasy team.

Aaron believes in conspiracy theories, yet somehow hasn't uncovered the biggest one of all: how the Dallas Cowboys keep convincing people they're a professional football team.

(Go Pack Go.)

Welcome to Week 7, where a former assistant manager turned sales rep faces a 1–5 Cowboys fan with anger issues and absolutely no championships to show for them.

Hunter (3–3) has won multiple fantasy titles, but this season he's sitting in fifth place with the second-lowest point total in the league. He transferred stores, stepped down from management, and apparently brought that same career trajectory into fantasy football.

At least he's consistent about moving backward.

Hunter also happens to be a hypochondriac who spends his evenings Googling symptoms of diseases he doesn't have, while overlooking the one genuine medical emergency in his life: his fantasy football team.

Aaron (1–5), meanwhile, is a certified hothead and conspiracy theorist whose beloved Cowboys recently got their asses kicked by Green Bay.

Naturally, Aaron is investigating. Were the referees compromised? Did the NFL coordinate the humiliation? Was the entire game an elaborate cover-up?

Or does Dallas just fucking suck?

Hunter's Eagles haven't exactly provided much comfort either. Between the two of them, they've spent more time defending disappointing football teams than celebrating victories.

When they're not working or watching their teams implode, both men are busy raising the next generation. Aaron watches his grandkids, Hunter takes care of his daughter, and both spend their free time hunting Pokémon cards like grown men competing with eight-year-olds for custody of a holographic Charizard.

Two responsible adults teaching children that sharing is important unless there's a rare Pokémon involved, in which case the little bastards can get jobs.

WEEK 7: A MEDICAL EMERGENCY WAITING TO HAPPEN

PROJECTED: AARON 135.16 | HUNTER 106.32

WIN ODDS: AARON 71% | HUNTER 29%

Aaron has scored 779.02 points compared with Hunter's 737.06, despite having two fewer victories. He also owns the league's single-week scoring record after dropping an absurd 201 points in Week 5.

Apparently, Aaron can score enough points to embarrass an entire league in one afternoon but can't figure out how to win more than one fucking game.

Hunter, meanwhile, has spent years collecting fantasy championships, only to find himself sitting at .500 with the second-lowest scoring total in the league.

For a man who worries about every possible health problem, it's remarkable he hasn't diagnosed his fantasy franchise with a terminal illness.

COMMISSIONER'S ANALYSIS & VERDICT

Aaron's Matthew Stafford, Puka Nacua, and Malik Nabers give him significant offensive firepower. Hunter counters with Dak Prescott, A.J. Brown, DeVonta Smith, and George Kittle, providing enough upside to make this competitive.

OFFICIAL PICK: AARON BY 28.84 POINTS.

A victory would move Aaron to 2–5 and give him something to celebrate besides a Pokémon card he probably wrestled away from a third-grader.

A loss would drop Hunter to 3–4, raising serious questions about how a former champion can spend so much time worrying about imaginary illnesses while ignoring the very real disease destroying his fantasy season.

One man has championship experience. The other has a historic scoring record. Both are currently being outperformed by people who probably don't know what a holographic Charizard is.

And when the final whistle blows, one of these grown men will return to his family victorious.

The other will return to his family, open a pack of Pokémon cards, and discover that even the fucking children have better luck than he does.
