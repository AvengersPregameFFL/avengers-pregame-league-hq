TOP STORY #4 · WEEK 6
SHE COULD, SO SHE DID.
ALLYSA SURVIVES JAKE
A WIN IS A WIN. BARELY.

Allysa narrowly defeated Jake, according to the commissioner. The first five weeks show just how surprising that close finish was: Allysa averaged 171.81 fantasy points per week compared with Jake’s 120.52. Allysa’s all-play record was 28–7; Jake’s was 6–29.

Jake arrived as the Short Bus Showdown champion after beating Jerry’s Justice League 146.40 to 135.82 in Week 4. But the following week he scored just 91.40, the league’s lowest total. Against that backdrop, Allysa’s narrow escape is hardly the commanding performance her season statistics suggested.

WOMAN OF THE WEEK, APPARENTLY: Just like Caitlyn Jenner, one of Glamour’s 2015 Women of the Year, rookie manager Allysa walked away with a headline. The difference? Allysa had to face Jake, who entered the week averaging 51.29 fewer fantasy points per game. She didn’t play up to her own numbers. She played down to her opponent and somehow turned what looked like a mismatch into a suspense thriller.

ANALYST VERDICT: Allysa earned the reported win. Jake gets another near-miss to add to his résumé. The precise Week 6 margin remains unverified in the finalized tracker.

She could, so she did. Jake almost could.
