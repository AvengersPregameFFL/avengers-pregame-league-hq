For much of the season, Nick has been running through Avengers Pregame like a man carrying a four-leaf clover, a rabbit's foot and the contact information of whoever makes the schedule.

Then Mike snapped his fingers.

Apparently, the secret to improving his fantasy football team was making it worse.

THE SCHEDULE FRAUD INVESTIGATION

Nick has faced the fewest opponent points in the entire league. Five of his eight victories came against opponents scoring fewer than 130 points. Once opponents cross that threshold, his winning percentage gets cut in half.

To be fair, Nick has produced some excellent performances himself. His record isn't entirely luck, but the numbers suggest he's received a generous amount of assistance.

Nick doesn't need the Infinity Stones. He's already got the Schedule Stone.

MIKE'S ROAD TO THE PLAYOFFS

Here's where things get interesting.

Through eleven weeks, Nick has scored just 1.86 more fantasy points than Mike. Their all-play records differ by only one win, yet Nick has two additional head-to-head victories.

The standings suggest Nick is comfortably superior. The underlying numbers suggest they're remarkably evenly matched.

Mike's next two games against Aaron and Travis could have significant postseason implications. Winning both would strengthen his playoff position while dealing two direct competitors valuable losses.

Mike doesn't need to eliminate half the universe. Just two fantasy football teams.
