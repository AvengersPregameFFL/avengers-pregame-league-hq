WEEK 7 · MATCHUP 2 · THE PREGAME
PRIME TIME TRAV VS. THE SHORT BUS SURVIVOR
God Helped Travis Once. God Gave Jake a Lawyer Wife and a Saltwater Pool. Apparently, Fantasy Football Was Where He Drew the Line.

Week 7 brings together two franchises with a combined 4–8 record and enough disappointment to qualify as a Tennessee Titans support group.

Travis (3–3) enters the week following what can only be described as a religious experience. His beloved Titans finally won a game, and after an entire season of incorrect prime-time predictions, he finally picked a winner.

One. Fucking. Game.

His breakthrough came courtesy of the 49ers, who nearly pissed away a victory against the shitty-ass Washington Commanders before escaping 23–17.

God apparently looked down, saw Travis sitting at zero correct prime-time picks, and decided to grant him one miracle. Not a second. Not a winning streak. Just enough divine intervention to prevent a grown man from going an entire season without correctly identifying a football team.

And because the universe apparently wasn't finished humiliating him, Travis's fantasy team also helped Mike snap his losing streak in Week 6.

For a man whose primary personality trait is being a smartass, the football gods have provided an extraordinary amount of material for everyone else.

Meanwhile, Jake (1–5) spent Week 6 nearly upsetting Allysa before being returned to his reserved seat at the back of the Short Bus.

Unlike Travis, Jake has clearly enjoyed God's generosity outside of football. The man has a wife who's a lawyer, a beautiful saltwater pool, and a comfortable life most people would kill for.

Apparently, when God was handing out blessings, Jake got everything except the ability to manage a fucking fantasy football team.

And the worst part? Jake remains relentlessly optimistic. Every Sunday he shows up smiling, convinced this is finally his week. It's the kind of unwavering faith normally associated with religious martyrs and people who keep buying lottery tickets after declaring bankruptcy.

His lone victory came against Ron in the legendary Short Bus Showdown, which Jake celebrated like he'd just liberated Europe. Unfortunately, the parade ended, reality returned, and his championship aspirations went straight back into the pool.

WEEK 7: THE NUMBERS AND THE NONSENSE

PROJECTED: TRAVIS 132.07 | JAKE 119.75

WIN ODDS: TRAVIS 66% | JAKE 34%

Travis brings Bijan Robinson, Derrick Henry, and Drake Maye, giving him the stronger projected foundation. Unfortunately, George Pickens and Parker Washington are on bye, proving that a man who spends Sundays criticizing NFL coaches still hasn't mastered checking a fucking calendar.

Jake counters with Mahomes, Jonathan Taylor, Nico Collins, and Travis Kelce — legitimate firepower undermined by DJ Moore being out and Bhayshul Tuten on bye.

Both managers have enough talent to win. Apparently, neither has enough supervision to set a complete lineup.

COMMISSIONER'S ANALYSIS & VERDICT

Travis has outscored Jake by 73.90 points through six weeks, and his backfield gives him the advantage. Jake's passing attack offers genuine upset potential, particularly with six-point passing touchdowns.

OFFICIAL PICK: TRAVIS BY 12.32 POINTS.

A victory moves Travis to 4–3 and gives him something else to be a smartass about. A Jake upset doubles his season win total and potentially triggers another championship celebration requiring adult supervision.

The real danger is Travis developing confidence after correctly picking one prime-time game. One more correct prediction and he'll be explaining defensive coverages to people who actually played football.

Jake, meanwhile, could finish dead last and still spend the offseason relaxing beside his saltwater pool, listening to his successful attorney wife discuss her day while he explains how injuries ruined his championship season.

God gave Travis one correct football pick. He gave Jake a life worth living.

Unfortunately, neither blessing appears to include a league championship.

And somewhere, Ron is watching, painfully aware that he's still the only motherfucker who managed to lose to Jake.
