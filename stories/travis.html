THE OTHER SIDE OF THE LEDGER

An actual sports analytics desk has to tell the whole story. Travis's 658.90 points through five weeks rank well below DK's 838.68 and Nick's 783.54. So the data supports some brutal close-game luck, not the claim that every loss was undeserved.

THE PRIME-TIME PARADOX

On the pick'em side, Travis's repeated prime-time misses aren't bad luck in the same sense as a 1.26-point fantasy loss. Those are incorrect predictions. His Week 6 Cowboys pick was another miss. The man can get victimized by a brutal fantasy matchup and still be responsible for picking the wrong football team. Two disasters. Two completely different statistical causes.

THE ANALYST DESK VERDICT

UNLUCKY MOMENTS? YES. UNLUCKIEST IN THE LEAGUE? NOT PROVEN. Travis has endured a painfully narrow defeat and faces tougher opponents than Nick. But his lower all-play performance limits the argument that fate alone explains his results. If he wins Monday, it's resilience. If he loses, the prime-time curse gets another chapter.
