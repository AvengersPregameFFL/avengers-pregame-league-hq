THE DK DIFFERENCE: OPPONENT-PROOF SCORING

Through five finalized weeks, DK scored 838.68 points—just 20.36 behind league scoring leader Allysa. His weekly totals: 182.26, 167.70, 165.94, 162.18 and 160.60. Five straight weeks over 160.
