THE SHORT BUS SHOWDOWN

Two Idiots. One Rivalry. Zero Expectations.

Throughout history, mankind has witnessed legendary rivalries. Batman versus Superman. Ali versus Frazier. Good versus evil.

And then there's Jake versus Ron.

A matchup so fucking terrible that historians will one day question whether it actually happened or if the entire league suffered a collective brain injury.

On one side stood Jake, manager of Loki Down Bad, a man whose fantasy football strategy resembles a toddler trying to operate a microwave. Jake had spent the season getting his ass kicked so consistently that the league considered giving him a participation trophy just for remembering his Sleeper password.

On the other side stood Ron, a man whose draft strategy apparently involved closing his eyes and pointing at random names. Ron entered the matchup with the confidence of a champion and the managerial instincts of a fucking traffic cone.

Neither man was particularly dangerous. Neither man appeared to understand how fantasy football worked.

But somebody had to win.

When kickoff arrived, Jake's team moved slower than Internet Explorer in 2003. Meanwhile, Ron's players performed like they'd just found out football was their actual profession.

Touchdowns were scarce. Competence was nonexistent. The Sleeper app reportedly considered shutting down the matchup out of embarrassment.

Even the fantasy football gods looked down and asked, "What the fuck are these two doing?"

But against all odds, something extraordinary happened.

JAKE FUCKING WON.

Church bells rang. Scientists abandoned their research. ESPN immediately investigated whether there had been a scoring error.

Jake celebrated like he'd won seven Super Bowls, defeated Thanos, and personally saved humanity.

Meanwhile, Ron was left with the most humiliating distinction imaginable.

He lost to fucking Jake.

But here's where this ridiculous story takes an unexpected turn.

THE PLOT TWIST

The very next week, through the grace of God and what can only be described as a glitch in the fantasy football universe, RON BROKE THE ALL-TIME LEAGUE RECORD FOR MOST POINTS SCORED IN A SINGLE GAME.

Yes. That Ron.

The same motherfucker who couldn't beat Jake suddenly transformed into the 2007 New England Patriots.

Scientists are investigating. ESPN is demanding answers. And Jake is probably still trying to figure out how he beat the guy who just rewrote the record books.

Apparently, losing to Jake was all Ron needed to unlock his final form.

And just like that, the Short Bus Showdown went from two clueless managers battling for survival to the birth of something truly special.

One week, Jake wins his first game. The next, Ron sets the league scoring record.

Two historic achievements. Two questionable managers. One rivalry that could define this league for years to come.

THE SHORT BUS SHOWDOWN: TWO MEN, ONE BRAINCELL, AND ABSOLUTELY NO FUCKING EXPLANATION.

THE CAROLINA CIVIL WAR

THE OFFICIAL RIVALRY ORIGIN

An Avengers Pregame Historical Documentary

Two States. Two Generals. One Psychological Disaster.

THE WEST VIRGINIA YEARS

Long before the Carolinas became a war zone, two future military disasters were growing up in West Virginia.

Nick, a product of Capital High School, developed a smart mouth and a remarkable ability to piss people off without actually trying.

Travis, a Hoover High School graduate, came from the hills, where apparently emotional stability was not part of the curriculum.

Their paths crossed through the future commissioner, who had known Travis since kindergarten and Nick since ninth grade.

Both played soccer goalkeeper growing up. Both played Madden and Call of Duty.

And both shared one important military tradition: rage-quitting whenever the commissioner started putting the belt to their asses.

Nick laughed things off.

Travis lost his goddamn mind.

It was the beginning of a beautiful friendship.

THE GREAT CAROLINA MIGRATION

Eventually, both men fled West Virginia.

Travis escaped to Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, apparently believing the ocean could wash away years of questionable decisions.

Nick relocated to Lake Norman, North Carolina, where his soon-to-be wife, a doctor, had purchased a beautiful house.

One man moved south to find a better life.

The other moved south and found a better mortgage situation.

Neither had conquered anything, but both now occupied opposing Carolina territories.

The stage was set.

THE SIX-WEEK PRESSURE COOKER

Then came the 2026 Avengers Pregame season.

Nick started 6–0.

Six victories. Six weeks of talking shit. Six weeks of Nick treating the standings like proof he was the greatest military commander since Napoleon.

And the worst part?

Nick wasn’t even trying to piss Travis off. He genuinely thought he was hilarious.

Meanwhile, South Carolina was approaching a state of emergency.

Travis was furious with his fantasy team.

Furious with his own decisions.

Furious with his picks.

And, as a Tennessee Titans fan, furious with a franchise that had spent years preparing him for disappointment.

Nick’s undefeated streak was the hand shaking an already pressurized Coke bottle.

Every joke added another shake.

Every victory tightened the cap.

And every Titans loss brought Travis one step closer to a complete emotional evacuation.

By Week 9, intelligence reports suggested the Southern general had at least one screw loose.

Possibly several.

The North had a comedian. The South had a ticking time bomb.

THE DECLARATION OF WAR

In Week 9 of 2026, Avengers Pregame officially recognized its second league rivalry:

THE CAROLINA CIVIL WAR.

Nick’s Flash 23 Blast versus Travis’s Fantastic Four Skin.

A conflict decades in the making, beginning in West Virginia, surviving years of video game rage-quits, and finally erupting across the Carolina border.

There would be no peace treaty.

Every future matchup would become another battle.

And the commissioner, who introduced these two idiots in the first place, could only watch the disaster unfold.

His assessment was simple:

“I’ve been beating both their asses since before either one had a Carolina to defend.”

THE NORTH HAS THE MOUTH. THE SOUTH HAS THE TEMPER. AND THIS WAR IS JUST GETTING STARTED.
