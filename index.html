Award Encyclopedia

What each award means and how it is determined.

Battle Beast

Highest fantasy points scored in a single week.

Automatic — highest actual lineup score.

Uncle Ben

Lowest fantasy points scored in a single week.

Automatic — lowest actual lineup score.

Watcher

Biggest lineup mistake measured by usable points left on the bench.

Automatic — optimal eligible lineup points minus actual points; review eligibility.

Doctor Strange

Best foresight, lineup call, or managerial decision of the week.

Commissioner selection.

Suicide Squad

Worst managerial decision of the week.

Commissioner selection; replaces Reverse Flash.

Great Power

A losing manager had a realistic winning lineup available but failed to start it.

Commissioner review; award only if a legal alternative could change the outcome.

Punisher

Largest winning margin of the week.

Automatic — winner’s points minus opponent’s points.

Goblin Glider

Largest fantasy scoring decline compared with the previous week.

Automatic — biggest week-to-week drop.

Invisible Woman

An eligible starting roster position was left empty.

Manual verification; an empty bench spot does not qualify.

Kryptonite

A starting player or defense scored negative fantasy points.

Manual verification of negative score in starting lineup.

14,000,605 Outcomes

A perfect NFL Pick’em week: every game selected correctly.

Automatic — correct picks equal all games played that week.

Stormbreaker

A manager ends the league’s longest active winning streak.

Award only when a meaningful league-leading streak is snapped.

The Joker

The most ridiculous, brutal, or painful fantasy loss of the week.

Commissioner selection.

Thanos

Most regular-season wins, but fails to win the league championship.

Season award determined after playoffs.

THE SHORT BUS SHOWDOWN

Two Idiots. One Rivalry. Zero Expectations.

Throughout history, mankind has witnessed legendary rivalries. Batman versus Superman. Ali versus Frazier. Good versus evil.

And then there's Jake versus Ron.

A matchup so fucking terrible that historians will one day question whether it actually happened or if the entire league suffered a collective brain injury.

On one side stood Jake, manager of Loki Down Bad, a man whose fantasy football strategy resembles a toddler trying to operate a microwave. Jake had spent the season getting his ass kicked so consistently that the league considered giving him a participation trophy just for remembering his Sleeper password.

On the other side stood Ron, a man whose draft strategy apparently involved closing his eyes and pointing at random names. Ron entered the matchup with the confidence of a champion and the managerial instincts of a fucking traffic cone.

Neither man was particularly dangerous. Neither man appeared to understand how fantasy football worked.

But somebody had to win.

When kickoff arrived, Jake's team moved slower than Internet Explorer in 2003. Meanwhile, Ron's players performed like they'd just found out football was their actual profession.

Touchdowns were scarce. Competence was nonexistent. The Sleeper app reportedly considered shutting down the matchup out of embarrassment.

Even the fantasy football gods looked down and asked, "What the fuck are these two doing?"

But against all odds, something extraordinary happened.

JAKE FUCKING WON.

Church bells rang. Scientists abandoned their research. ESPN immediately investigated whether there had been a scoring error.

Jake celebrated like he'd won seven Super Bowls, defeated Thanos, and personally saved humanity.

Meanwhile, Ron was left with the most humiliating distinction imaginable.

He lost to fucking Jake.

But here's where this ridiculous story takes an unexpected turn.

THE PLOT TWIST

The very next week, through the grace of God and what can only be described as a glitch in the fantasy football universe, RON BROKE THE ALL-TIME LEAGUE RECORD FOR MOST POINTS SCORED IN A SINGLE GAME.

Yes. That Ron.

The same motherfucker who couldn't beat Jake suddenly transformed into the 2007 New England Patriots.

Scientists are investigating. ESPN is demanding answers. And Jake is probably still trying to figure out how he beat the guy who just rewrote the record books.

Apparently, losing to Jake was all Ron needed to unlock his final form.

And just like that, the Short Bus Showdown went from two clueless managers battling for survival to the birth of something truly special.

One week, Jake wins his first game. The next, Ron sets the league scoring record.

Two historic achievements. Two questionable managers. One rivalry that could define this league for years to come.

THE SHORT BUS SHOWDOWN: TWO MEN, ONE BRAINCELL, AND ABSOLUTELY NO FUCKING EXPLANATION.
